ShippingExplorer Support

AI-powered customer support agent. Click the chat button in the bottom-right corner to try it out.

How to embed on your site

Add this single script tag before the closing </body> tag:

<script>window.SE_SUPPORT_API = "https://shipping-support-agent.onrender.com";</script>
<script src="https://shipping-support-agent.onrender.com/static/widget.js"></script>

Test scenarios
